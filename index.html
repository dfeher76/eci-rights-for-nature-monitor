1 Threshold: the minimum number of statements of support required in each member state, as published by the European Commission in the register of the initiative.

2 Target: 1,100,000 divided by the total number of people with voting rights in the European Parliament elections in the EU, multiplied by the number of such people in the respective country. This spreads the campaign goal of 1.1 million signatures in proportion to the electorate. The figures are the numbers of people eligible to vote in the 2024 European Parliament elections from the official Eurostat table demo_popep, last updated on 2 August 2024, about 358 million in total. Total population would be used where these figures are not available, which is not the case for any member state. For Germany, Eurostat counts about 61.9 million, while the national authority states up to 64.9 million because it also includes EU citizens who still have to register to vote.

In the relative view, each bar is as long as the larger of its goals, so the filled part shows the progress towards the goal. Use the absolute view to compare the numbers between countries.

Source: European Commission, official register of the initiative. These are online signatures as reported by the EC collection system. They have not yet been verified by the national authorities, and paper signatures are not included. Times are shown as published by the EC, in Brussels time.
